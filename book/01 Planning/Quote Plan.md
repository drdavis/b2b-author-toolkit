# Quote Plan

Which interview voices go in which chapter, so the book doesn't lean on the same two people everywhere and every interviewee's best material lands where it's strongest. `/quote-plan` builds and refreshes this page; `/brief` and `/draft` follow it.

## Rules this plan follows
- **One key quote at the top of every chapter:** an interviewee's exact words, used nowhere else in the book.
- **One anchor per chapter:** the person whose story opens it.
- **At most two or three supporting interview voices per chapter.** Online and community voices go in "Has this happened to you?" and don't count toward this limit.
- **Names only with consent.** Until someone says yes, their quote is credited by role, for example "a fractional CFO who left a big firm two years ago".
- **Every quote here is checked word for word against its transcript** by the quote checker.
