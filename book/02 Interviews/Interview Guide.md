# Interview Guide

The questions for every book interview. `/prep-interview` copies these into a prep note and adds follow-ups for the chapters that need evidence most. Rewrite them to fit your book; keep the shape.

## Before you start
- Ask for consent first: may you record, quote them, and use their name? Note the answer and the timestamp.
- Don't assume they're struggling. Ask about what happened, not opinions. Every answer is useful.

## 1. Who they are
- Tell me about your work and who you do it for.
- How long have you been doing it, and how did you get started?

## 2. How work finds them
- Think about your last three clients. How did each one find you?
- What's changed about that in the last couple of years?

## 3. What they've tried
- What have you tried to get in front of the right people? What happened?
- What did you stop doing, and why?

## 4. The moment
- Tell me about a time it really worked, or really didn't. Walk me through it.

## 5. Looking ahead
- If a friend in your position asked what to do this year, what would you tell them?

## Closing
- What would you expect a book on this to tell you?
- Is there anyone else I should talk to?
