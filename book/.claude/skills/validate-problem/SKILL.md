Before anything else, read `00 Start Here.md`, `01 Planning/Decisions.md`, `01 Planning/Book Status.md` and the last five lines of `01 Planning/Session Log.md`. Follow every rule in CLAUDE.md. Do this one job only. Also read `01 Planning/Book Brief.md`, `Research/Problem Validation.md`, the coded findings in `02 Interviews/`, the latest note in `Research/Online/Runs/` and the Quote Bank.

Job: score the problem the book is built on. Best run after the first three to five interviews, then again whenever the evidence grows.

1. **Score each test** (Strong, Plausible, Mixed, Unproven), each with its best evidence as exact quotes and sources:
   - **Painful:** do people describe it with feeling, in their own words?
   - **Pervasive:** do many people in the reader group have it?
   - **Priority now:** is it near the top of their list this year?
   - **Pay:** have they spent money or time trying to fix it, and what happened?
   Rate only on evidence in the folder. Where there isn't any, the rating is Unproven.
2. **The words readers use.** List the exact phrases people use for the problem. Note where those differ from the words in the Book Brief; the book should lead with the words readers feel, not the label the author uses.
3. **Bottom line** in three sentences: how strong the problem is, where it's weakest, and what that means for the book's framing.
4. **Open questions** that would move the weakest test, worded for the Interview Guide. Add them to `02 Interviews/Interview Guide.md` under the section they fit, marked *Validation:*, if the author agrees.
5. Add a Change log line. If the evidence suggests changing the book's promise or framing, say so to the author; don't change the Book Brief yourself.

When finished, update `01 Planning/Book Status.md` and `00 Start Here.md`, and add one line to `01 Planning/Session Log.md` (date | job | what was done | where it stopped | next). Then tell the author in three lines or fewer what you did and what comes next.
