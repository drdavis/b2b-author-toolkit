Before anything else, read `00 Start Here.md`, `01 Planning/Decisions.md`, `01 Planning/Book Status.md` and the last five lines of `01 Planning/Session Log.md`. Follow every rule in CLAUDE.md. Do this one job only.

Job: write the brief for Chapter **$ARGUMENTS**. Don't draft the chapter.

Use the chapter note: start from `## The chapter pattern`, then draw on `## From interviews`, `## From research` and `## From the author`, plus the Quote Bank and any Parking Lot rows for this chapter (mark a row "used in ChNN brief" when you pull it in). Follow this chapter's row in `01 Planning/Quote Plan.md` (key quote, anchor, supporting voices) if it has one. Prefer interview and author stories over online ones where both exist. Name an interviewee only if their consent is yes. Fill the `## Brief` section:

- **The one idea** (one sentence)
- **Opening story** (who, what happened, source file) or `[STORY NEEDED: ...]`
- **Has this happened to you?** three to five reader quotes, exact, with sources
- **How it works** the steps, and the worked example
- **This week** one action under an hour
- **The takeaway** one line
- **Bonus** what the reader gets for this chapter, if anything (add new ideas to `Bonuses/Bonus Status.md`)
- **Gaps** anything marked `[STORY NEEDED]` or `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`

Check the brief against the quality gate in `01 Planning/How We Write This Book.md` and say whether it passes, and why not if it doesn't. Set the chapter's `layer` to `brief`.

When finished, update `01 Planning/Book Status.md` and `00 Start Here.md`, and add one line to `01 Planning/Session Log.md` (date | job | what was done | where it stopped | next). Then tell the author in three lines or fewer what you did and what comes next.
