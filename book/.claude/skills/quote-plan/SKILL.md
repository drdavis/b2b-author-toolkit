Before anything else, read `00 Start Here.md`, `01 Planning/Decisions.md`, `01 Planning/Book Status.md` and the last five lines of `01 Planning/Session Log.md`. Follow every rule in CLAUDE.md. Do this one job only. Also read `01 Planning/Quote Plan.md`, the Book Blueprint's Interview roster and Evidence map (not the whole file), and the `## Coded findings` of every interview note in `02 Interviews/`.

Job: build or refresh the Quote Plan.

1. **Sum up each call in one line**, and name the chapters where it's strongest. Fill `## What each call is really about`.
2. **Place the voices.** For each chapter, choose:
   - one **key quote** that opens it: exact words with the timestamp, strong enough to stand alone, and not used in any other chapter;
   - one **anchor**: the person whose story opens the chapter;
   - up to two or three **supporting voices**.
   Spread people across the book. If one person is anchoring more than two chapters, or appears in more than half of them, move some of their material to where someone else is weaker. Prefer people with consent yes for anchors.
3. **Respect what's settled.** Keep any anchor or key quote already confirmed in `Decisions.md`. Show the author your proposed changes as a table (chapter, was, now, why) and wait for their OK before writing.
4. **Write** `## Chapter by chapter`, list anything unplaced under `## Open`, and add a Change log line.
5. Where a chapter already has a draft and its anchor or key quote changed, add a revise line to that chapter note's `## Still needed`.
6. Run the quote checker and fix anything it lists.

When finished, update `01 Planning/Book Status.md` and `00 Start Here.md`, and add one line to `01 Planning/Session Log.md` (date | job | what was done | where it stopped | next). Then tell the author in three lines or fewer what you did and what comes next.
